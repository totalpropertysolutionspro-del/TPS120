The Hidden Costs of In-House Janitorial Nobody Talks About

The table above hits the obvious line items. But anyone who's actually managed facilities knows the real drain comes from the stuff that never shows up on a budget spreadsheet:

Turnover Is a Killer

Janitorial positions have some of the highest turnover rates in any organization — routinely 75-100% annually in the Capital Region. Every time your janitor quits, you're looking at: 2-3 weeks without coverage, $500-1,200 in job posting and screening costs, manager time spent interviewing (at $40-60/hour), and training the new hire on your space. Multiply that by even one turnover per year and you're burning money and management attention on a role that should be invisible.

You're Now a Janitorial Supply Chain Manager

Did you know industrial floor cleaner comes in 17 different formulations? That the wrong pH on your VCT tile strips the finish? When you hire in-house, you're suddenly responsible for sourcing, stocking, and managing cleaning chemicals, equipment maintenance, vacuum belts, mop heads, microfiber cloths, trash liners — the list goes on. TPS Pro absorbs all of that. You get a clean building. Period.

Coverage Gaps Will Happen

Your janitor takes a week off in August. Who cleans the building? Your janitor calls in sick on Tuesday. Who covers? Most businesses either pay overtime to stretch someone else thin, or they let the building slide for a few days — and employees notice. Professional janitorial services have built-in redundancy. If one crew member is out, another fills in. You never even know.

Quality Is Only as Good as Your Last Hire

One great janitor can make a building shine. One mediocre janitor who cuts corners — and you might not notice for weeks until the complaints roll in. With an outsourced cleaning partner, quality is contractually guaranteed and consistently supervised. You're not betting your building's appearance on a single person's work ethic.

When In-House Janitorial Actually Makes Sense

To be fair, there are scenarios where hiring your own janitorial staff is the right call:

  • 24/7 operational facilities — manufacturing plants, data centers, or hospitals where cleaning needs to happen continuously throughout shifts
  • Highly specialized environments — cleanrooms, pharmaceutical manufacturing, or labs requiring full-time, on-site personnel with niche training
  • Very large single-site campuses — 100,000+ sq ft facilities where you'd need a dedicated team of 3-4 anyway
  • Security-sensitive government facilities — where background clearance requirements make contractor rotation impractical

For the other 90% of Albany businesses — offices, retail stores, medical practices, apartment buildings, warehouses, professional suites — outsourcing is the clear financial winner. And the cleaner building is a bonus.
