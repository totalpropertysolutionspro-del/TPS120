What Floor Stripping & Waxing Actually Is

Mopping cleans the surface of a floor. It does not remove what's trapped in the floor. Over months of traffic, the wax or finish on your VCT tile, terrazzo, or hardwood absorbs dirt, grime, and scuff marks. Each new layer of wax seals the old grime in. The result: floors that look cloudy, gray, or yellow even immediately after cleaning.

Floor stripping removes that tired, contaminated finish down to the bare surface. Then a fresh sealer and multiple coats of wax are applied and buffed to a clean, protective shine. It's not a cosmetic touch-up — it's resurfacing that protects the floor material underneath and extends its life by years.

5 Signs Your Albany Building Needs Floor Stripping & Waxing

  • Floors look dull right after mopping. If the shine is gone even after cleaning, the finish itself is worn.
  • Yellowing or gray haze on VCT tile. A classic sign of wax buildup trapping dirt — common in high-traffic lobbies and hallways.
  • Scuff marks that won't come out. Black heel marks and cart tracks that resist scrubbing are embedded in the finish.
  • Wax buildup at the edges. Thick, discolored finish collecting near baseboards means too many layers have accumulated without a proper strip.
  • Finish worn through to bare tile. Once the protective layer is gone, the floor material itself starts to deteriorate — and repairs cost far more than a strip and wax.
