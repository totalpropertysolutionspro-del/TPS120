What Is a Day Porter?

A day porter is a cleaning professional stationed at your facility during operating hours — not after hours like a traditional janitorial crew. They handle everything that comes up throughout the day: spills, restroom restocking, lobby upkeep, trash runs, and high-touch surface cleaning. Think of them as your facility's on-site maintenance presence while the building is alive and active.

Unlike an overnight janitorial service that handles the deep clean after everyone leaves, a day porter maintains the building while people are in it. The two work together — overnight crew handles the heavy lifting, day porter keeps it presentable all day long.

Most Albany cleaning companies only offer after-hours cleaning. TPS Pro is one of the few providers in the Capital Region with dedicated day porter service — and we don't subcontract it out.

What Does a Day Porter Actually Do?

A day porter's job isn't one-size-fits-all. It's customized to your facility's traffic patterns and pain points. Here's what day porter service typically covers in an Albany commercial setting:

Restroom Upkeep (the big one)

  • Restock soap, paper towels, and toilet paper — before they run out
  • Wipe down counters, sinks, and mirrors multiple times per shift
  • Spot-mop floors after spills or heavy traffic
  • Empty trash before it overflows
  • Quick disinfect of high-touch surfaces (door handles, faucets, flush handles)

Common Area & Lobby Maintenance

  • Sweep and mop entryways — first impressions count
  • Clean glass doors and windows of smudges and fingerprints
  • Tidy reception seating areas, straighten magazines
  • Empty exterior cigarette urns and sweep entry mats
  • Respond to spills immediately — coffee, water, whatever

Break Room & Kitchen Areas

  • Wipe down counters and tables between lunch waves
  • Empty trash and recycling — nobody wants 2 PM garbage smell
  • Restock paper goods and hand soap
  • Quick-sweep floors after meal periods

Special Requests (this is where we shine)

  • Conference room setup and reset between meetings
  • Event support — pre-event cleaning, during-event upkeep
  • Snow and salt tracking cleanup at entrances (winter in Albany — you know)
  • Mailroom and package area tidying
  • Minor maintenance flagging — notice a burned-out light, loose door handle, or leak? Your day porter reports it before it becomes a problem
