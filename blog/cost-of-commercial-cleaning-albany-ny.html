What Actually Drives the Price

The number on your quote isn't random. Here's what moves it — and where you can save.

1. Square Footage (But It's Not Everything)

Yes, bigger spaces cost more. But a 5,000 sq ft warehouse with polished concrete floors is cheaper to clean than a 5,000 sq ft medical office with exam rooms, waiting areas, and HIPAA-level sanitation requirements. The type of square footage matters more than the number. Commercial cleaning in Albany typically runs $0.08–$0.25 per square foot per visit depending on use case.

2. Frequency — The Biggest Lever You Have

Daily service costs way less per visit than weekly. Here's why: our crew already knows your building, the supply chain is consistent, and there's less deep-cleaning buildup. A 5x/week contract can drop your per-visit cost by 30–40% versus 1x/week. If you're on the fence about frequency, going more often is cheaper per clean — and your space stays cleaner.

3. Specialty Requirements

Medical facilities, labs, food-service areas, and childcare centers need specific protocols, products, and sometimes documentation. That adds cost — typically 20–40% above standard commercial rates. Green cleaning with certified eco-friendly products? At TPS Pro, it's the same price as standard. Most competitors charge a premium. Read that again.

4. Contract vs. One-Time

A recurring contract locks in better rates and guarantees availability. One-time jobs (post-construction, event cleanup, move-out) cost more because there's no ongoing relationship to spread costs across. If you're a property manager with a portfolio, volume contracts are your best friend — the per-unit rate drops significantly at 10+ units.

How to Get the Best Price on Commercial Cleaning in Albany

  1. Commit to a contract. Even 3 months locks in better pricing than month-to-month. A 12-month agreement typically saves 10–15%.
  2. Bundle services. Need commercial cleaning plus window cleaning? Floor stripping and waxing twice a year? Bundle it. Single-vendor packages are cheaper than multiple vendors.
  3. Go higher frequency. 5x/week costs less per clean than 3x/week. Counterintuitive but true — consistency drops the per-visit overhead.
  4. Ask about volume discounts. If you have multiple locations or manage a portfolio, you should be paying less per square foot. Period.
  5. Compare total cost, not just the line item. A cheaper quote that misses things (supplies not included, no weekend coverage, 60-day cancellation notice buried in the fine print) isn't cheaper. Look at the full picture.
