Prep Work — Where the Real Job Happens

The difference between a paint job that lasts 10 years and one that's peeling in two is almost always prep. TPS Pro doesn't cut corners on surface preparation — it's the foundation of everything we do. Before we pick up a brush or roller, your surfaces are clean, repaired, primed, and ready.

For interior painting jobs in Albany, NY, that means patching nail holes and dings, sanding glossy surfaces for adhesion, applying primer on fresh drywall or stain-blocking primer where needed, and protecting your floors and furniture with proper drop cloths and tape. For exterior jobs, it means scraping failed paint, caulking gaps around windows and trim, filling cracks, and pressure washing away years of grime before we ever open a can of paint.

  • Surface washing and degreasing
  • Scraping and sanding loose or failed paint
  • Caulking and gap filling on exteriors
  • Drywall repair and skim coating
  • Stain-blocking primer for water stains or smoke damage
  • Full floor and furniture protection on interior jobs
