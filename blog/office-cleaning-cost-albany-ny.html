What Office Cleaning Costs in Albany (Real Ranges)

Here's what businesses across the Capital Region actually pay for professional office cleaning in 2026. These are real starting ranges, not marketing fluff:

  • Small office (under 2,000 sq ft): $150–$350/month for 2–3 visits per week
  • Mid-size office (2,000–10,000 sq ft): $400–$1,500/month, 3–5 visits per week
  • Large office / multi-floor (10,000+ sq ft): $2,000–$6,000+/month with daily service

The single biggest factor isn't the cleaner — it's frequency. A five-night-a-week contract costs more than a twice-a-week contract, but it keeps your space consistently presentable and eliminates the expensive "deep clean catch-up" cycles that show up after months of neglect. The 5 Factors That Drive Your Office Cleaning Price

  • Square footage and layout. More space and more rooms = more time. Open floor plans clean faster than a maze of offices and conference rooms.
  • Frequency. Daily, three times a week, or once a week — this is the biggest lever on your monthly cost.
  • Scope of work. Restrooms, kitchens, and high-touch disinfection take longer than vacuuming and trash removal alone.
  • Facility type. Medical and dental offices need hospital-grade disinfectants and stricter protocols, which add cost — and protect your patients.
  • Specialty services. Floor stripping and waxing, interior window cleaning, and day porter coverage are priced separately or bundled in.

Where Albany Businesses Overpay (and Underpay)

The overpay trap: national franchises with a call center, a rotating crew, and a contract full of hidden fees. You pay a premium for a brand name, not better cleaning. The underpay trap: the "$99/month" guy who skips disinfection, skips restocking, and disappears the first time you complain. That's not savings — that's deferred damage.

Then there's the in-house trap. Hiring a part-time janitor sounds simple until you add salary, payroll taxes, benefits, supplies, equipment, training, and coverage for sick days and vacation. Most Albany businesses save 40–60% outsourcing vs. hiring in-house staff. Run the numbers yourself here.
