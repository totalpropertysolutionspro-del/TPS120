⚡ The 7-Question Commercial Cleaning Checklist

  1. Are you licensed, insured, and bonded in New York State?

1. Are You Licensed, Insured, and Bonded in New York State?

This is non-negotiable. If a cleaning company can't show you proof of general liability insurance, workers' compensation, and a surety bond, walk away. In New York, if an uninsured cleaner gets hurt on your property, you could be liable — not them. A bonded company also protects you against theft or property damage.

Any legitimate commercial cleaning company in Albany will have this documentation ready to share. If they hesitate or deflect, that's your red flag. TPS Pro carries full general liability insurance and workers' comp coverage — ask to see it.

How to Compare Quotes: The Real Math

You've got three quotes in front of you. Company A: $600/month. Company B: $850/month. Company C (TPS Pro): somewhere in the middle. Here's how to compare them fairly — beyond the headline number:

  • Divide by visits, not months. $600/month for 2x/week cleaning = $75/visit. $850/month for 3x/week = $70.83/visit. The "cheaper" quote is actually more expensive per clean.
  • Check what's excluded. Trash removal? Supplies? Floor buffing? Restroom restocking? "Full service" doesn't mean the same thing to every company. Get it in writing.
  • Factor in reliability. A $75/visit service that no-shows once a month costs you more in disruption than a $90/visit service that's never late. Ask about their no-show policy and backup plan.
  • Calculate the in-house alternative. Hiring one full-time janitor in Albany costs $35K-$45K/year in salary alone — plus benefits, payroll taxes, supplies, equipment, and coverage for sick days and vacation. That's why most businesses save $40K+ by outsourcing.
